ACROCARE – CENTRE FOR HAPPINESS & WELL-BEING 
CMHSI SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT SPECIFICATION 
Questionnaire, Scoring Engine, Critical-Safety Workflow & Automated Reporting 
1. System Overview 
The system should support the following workflow: 
1. ​ Collect student information and 54 questionnaire responses. 
2. ​ Store the original text response and its corresponding numeric score. 
3. ​ Calculate the nine domain scores automatically. 
4. ​ Calculate the combined descriptive score. 
5. ​ Assign sample-referenced relative bands where applicable. 
6. ​ Run a separate critical-item safety check. 
7. ​ Generate an internal counsellor/admin dashboard. 
8. ​ Generate a standardized individual student report/PDF. 
9. ​ Maintain follow-up, safety-review, referral, and professional-note records.

5. Score Calculation Rules 
·         Convert each response using the response-to-score table. 
·         For each domain, sum the six item scores assigned to that domain. 
·         Domain Score = sum of six domain item scores. 
·         Each domain has a maximum score of 24. 
·         Combined Score = sum of all 54 numeric item scores. 
·         Combined score has a possible range of 0–216. 
·         The domain profile must remain the primary basis for interpretation. 
·         The combined score must be labelled descriptive only. 
Example: If Anxiety responses are Rarely, Never, Rarely, Rarely, Sometimes, Sometimes, the numeric conversion is 1 + 0 + 1 + 1 + 2 + 2 = 7, 
therefore Anxiety = 7/24.

9. Recommended Software Architecture 
Keep the software in three separate layers: 
Layer 
Contents 
1. RAW DATA 
54 original responses; student information; administration metadata 
2. SCORING ENGINE 
Text-to-numeric conversion; item-to-domain mapping; domain 
scores; bands; critical-item logic 
3. REPORT ENGINE 
Scores; interpretations; priority areas; charts; follow-up section; PDF 
generation 
  
This separation will allow AcroCare to change report design later without accidentally changing the scoring algorithm. 
10. Domain-wise recommendations based on Lower, Mild, Moderate and Higher 
Relative bands  
1. Anxiety (AN) 
Band / situation 
Automated recommendation 
Lower Relative 
Continue healthy routines, adequate sleep, relaxation practices, and supportive social connections. Encourage awareness 
of situations that contribute to worry. 
Mild Relative 
Encourage slow breathing, grounding exercises, structured routines, and identification of worry triggers. Psychoeducation 
and optional counselling may be offered if concerns persist. 
Moderate Relative 
Recommend a counsellor discussion to explore the nature, frequency, and impact of anxiety. Encourage coping strategies, 
relaxation practice, sleep regulation, and monitoring of symptoms. 
Higher Relative 
Recommend timely professional review to understand the intensity and functional impact of anxiety. Develop an 
individualized support plan addressing coping skills, sleep, daily functioning, and stressors. 
  
2. Depression (DP) 
Band / situation 
Automated recommendation 
Lower Relative 
Encourage meaningful activities, healthy routines, supportive relationships, and regular self-care. 
Mild Relative 
Encourage daily routines, appropriate physical activity, positive social engagement, enjoyable activities, and awareness 
of mood changes. Offer psychoeducation and optional counselling. 
Moderate Relative 
Recommend a counsellor discussion to explore mood, energy, concentration, interest in activities, and impact on daily 
functioning. Encourage structured activity planning and social support. 
Higher Relative 
Recommend timely professional assessment and individualized support to explore persistent mood-related concerns and 
functional impact. Offer counselling or referral based on professional review. 
  
4. Suicidal Ideation (SI) 
Band / situation 
Automated recommendation 
Lower Relative 
Encourage emotional support, healthy coping strategies, and awareness of available campus counselling resources. 
Maintain well-being check-ins where appropriate. 
Mild Relative 
Recommend a supportive counsellor conversation to understand the context of reported thoughts, emotional distress, and 
available support resources. Encourage help-seeking and monitoring. 
Moderate Relative 
Recommend prompt professional review to explore the nature, frequency, context, and impact of suicidal thoughts. 
Assess safety and support needs directly. 
Higher Relative 
Recommend urgent professional safety assessment and immediate follow-up. The appropriate response should be 
determined through direct assessment and institutional protocol. 
  
5. Substance Abuse (SA)  
Band / situation 
Automated recommendation 
Any reported concern 
Encourage a confidential, non-judgmental counsellor discussion to understand the nature, frequency, context, 
and functional impact of substance use. Provide psychoeducation on healthier coping strategies. 
Use affects functioning 
Recommend professional review and individualized support to explore patterns, triggers, consequences, and 
available referral options. 
Student wants to reduce/stop 
Offer supportive guidance, motivational conversations, and referral to an appropriate qualified professional or 
de-addiction service where indicated. 
  
6. Self-Esteem Problems (SE) 
Band / situation 
Automated recommendation 
Lower Relative 
Encourage self-awareness, recognition of strengths, healthy self-talk, and meaningful activities. 
Mild Relative 
Encourage identifying strengths, challenging negative self-judgments, setting realistic goals, and accepting constructive 
feedback. 
Moderate Relative 
Recommend counselling support to explore negative self-perceptions, self-comparison, confidence concerns, and their 
impact on functioning. 
Higher Relative 
Recommend individualized professional support to explore persistent self-worth concerns and negative self-beliefs. 
Encourage gradual strength-building and supportive goal-setting. 
  
7. Interpersonal Problems (IP) 
Band / situation 
Automated recommendation 
Lower Relative 
Encourage healthy relationships, open communication, mutual respect, and positive social activities. 
Mild Relative 
Encourage active listening, assertive communication, boundary-setting, and seeking supportive connections. 
Moderate Relative 
Recommend a counsellor discussion to explore relationship difficulties, loneliness, trust concerns, communication 
patterns, and their impact on well-being. 
Higher Relative 
Recommend individualized counselling support for persistent interpersonal concerns, social isolation, trust 
difficulties, or relationship-related distress. 
  
8. Family Problems (FP) 
Band / situation 
Automated recommendation 
Lower Relative 
Encourage open family communication, mutual understanding, supportive relationships, and healthy personal 
boundaries. 
Mild Relative 
Encourage constructive communication, identifying manageable sources of family stress, and seeking support from 
trusted individuals. 
Moderate Relative 
Recommend a counsellor discussion to explore family stress, expectations, communication difficulties, and their impact 
on emotional well-being. 
Higher Relative 
Recommend individualized professional support to understand persistent family-related distress and identify safe, 
practical coping and support options. Consider family involvement only when appropriate. 
  
9. Academic Stress (AS) 
Band / situation 
Automated recommendation 
Lower Relative 
Encourage effective study routines, realistic academic goals, balanced schedules, and healthy breaks. 
Mild Relative 
Encourage time management, prioritization, planned study sessions, short breaks, and academic clarification or 
support when required. 
Moderate Relative 
Recommend a counsellor discussion to explore workload, expectations, performance concerns, and time-management 
difficulties. Encourage a structured academic action plan. 
Higher Relative 
Recommend timely professional and academic support to understand the impact of academic stress. Develop an 
individualized plan addressing workload, prioritization, expectations, and institutional resources. 
  
10. Career Concerns (CC) 
Band / situation 
Automated recommendation 
Lower Relative 
Encourage career exploration, awareness of interests and strengths, and gradual planning for future goals. 
Mild Relative 
Encourage exploring interests, gathering reliable information, identifying strengths, and discussing options with a career 
counsellor, mentor, or faculty member. 
Moderate Relative 
Recommend structured career guidance to explore uncertainty, decision-making difficulties, interests, abilities, and 
realistic options. Break decisions into manageable steps. 
Higher Relative 
Recommend timely individualized career counselling to explore persistent career-related distress, decision pressure, 
uncertainty, and impact on functioning. Develop a structured career-planning support plan. 
   
11. Software Implementation Requirement 
·         Calculate each domain score first. 
·         Assign the applicable relative band using approved thresholds. 
·         Retrieve only the recommendation corresponding to the obtained band. 
·         Display recommendations for all relevant domains, not only the highest-scoring domain. 
·         Do not assign a relative band to Substance Abuse. 
·         Run critical-item logic independently from routine recommendations. 
·         Use non-diagnostic and non-stigmatizing language. 
·         Allow counsellors to add professional notes or supplementary recommendations after review.
